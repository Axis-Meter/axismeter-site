# Product Marketing Context — Axis Meter Solutions

*Last updated: Feb 17, 2026*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Utility submetering for property owners who want to increase NOI and stop subsidizing tenant waste.
**What it does:** Axis installs, owns, and manages electricity and water submeters in multi-unit buildings. Tenants get billed for what they actually use. Property owners recover utility costs, increase property value, and get free leak/flood detection included with every install.
**Product category:** Utility submetering services (how customers search: "submetering companies", "utility submetering Ontario", "submeter installation")
**Product type:** B2B service with hardware (meters) + ongoing billing/management
**Business model:** 20-year agreement. $0 upfront for property owner. Axis owns equipment during term. Revenue from tenant delivery fee ($20-26/month per unit). Equipment stays with building at end of term.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Property owners are paying bulk utility bills and splitting costs evenly across units — subsidizing heavy users and eating into NOI.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big submetering companies (Metergy, Wyse, CARMA) focus on 200+ unit towers and ignore smaller landlords
- They're corporate and slow — you deal with call centers, not people
- None of them include leak detection standard
- Complex onboarding, unclear timelines, hidden costs
**What it costs them:** A 20-unit building can lose $2,000-4,000/month in unrecovered utility costs. A single undetected water leak can cause $50K+ in damage.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration watching utility bills climb while tenants waste energy. Fear of a catastrophic leak. Uncertainty about whether submetering is "worth the hassle."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:**
- Metergy Solutions — 900K+ meters, Brookfield-backed. Falls short: massive/corporate, slow processes, no personal touch, no free leak detection, ignores small landlords
- Wyse Meter — Canadian, sustainability-focused. Falls short: Canada-only, diversifying into EV/HVAC (unfocused), corporate feel
- CARMA Corp — 48 years old, heritage brand. Falls short: dated website/tech, repetitive messaging, minimal US presence, no leak detection emphasis

**Secondary:**
- Utility companies offering basic metering — limited to one utility type, no billing services
- DIY submetering (buy meters, manage yourself) — complex, no billing support, compliance risk

**Indirect:**
- RUBS (Ratio Utility Billing Systems) — allocates costs by square footage, not actual usage. Unfair, tenants hate it, doesn't drive conservation
- "Just include utilities in rent" — losing money every month, no conservation incentive
